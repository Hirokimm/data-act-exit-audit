## Included rights under the final annual order

Subject to accepted written terms, the final add-on is intended to permit derived licensed-dataset insights in:

- client-specific written reports or memoranda;
- client-specific presentations;
- derived charts or tables; and
- internal advisory workpapers supporting client deliverables.

The reservation itself does not activate these rights. Rights activate only after the final annual order, accepted written terms, and required payment are completed.

## Excluded uses

The reservation and final add-on do not permit:

- redistribution of the raw dataset;
- sublicensing;
- resale of dataset access;
- a white-label data portal or API;
- bulk public republication;
- use for more than 5,000 named end-client organizations without separate written scope; or
- use outside accepted written terms.

The offer does not add organizations to the underlying dataset, custom research, implementation work, or a faster delivery cadence.
