## Permitted derived outputs

Subject to the accepted written terms, the add-on is intended to permit the licensee to incorporate derived insights into:

- client-specific reports and memoranda;
- client presentations;
- derived charts and tables; and
- supporting advisory workpapers.

The licensee remains responsible for its client relationships, professional judgments, representations, and final deliverables.

## Excluded uses

The add-on does not include:

- redistribution of the raw dataset;
- sublicensing;
- resale of dataset access;
- a white-label portal or API;
- bulk public republication;
- use for more than 50,000 named end-client organizations without a separate written scope;
- use outside the accepted written terms;
- additional dataset organizations;
- custom research;
- implementation work; or
- a faster delivery cadence.
