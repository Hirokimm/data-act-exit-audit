# Client-Deliverable Reuse Global — Quote and Invoice Request Kit

Use this public-safe kit to move a selected Client-Deliverable Reuse Global tier from internal approval to a private quotation, vendor-onboarding, and reservation-invoice workflow.

The kit covers the 1K, 2.5K, 5K, 10K, and 20K paid-reservation tiers for organizations expecting to serve 501–20,000 distinct named end-client organizations annually.

After eligibility and scope are confirmed, written terms are accepted, and the reservation payment clears, the matching annual price is locked for 30 calendar days. The final annual order must be executed during that period; later pricing may apply after expiry.

- bank-detail verification process;

- required invoice fields and supporting documents;
- tax-withholding or certificate requirements;
- payment method, payment term, and approval deadline;

- internal quote, purchase-order, or contract reference.

Availability and applicability of requested vendor documents must be confirmed privately. This public kit does not represent that every requested document or buyer-specific term will be accepted.

Before submitting the unified request, confirm that:

- intended outputs are described without confidential client information;
- invoice intent and procurement route are stated;

- confidential billing, legal, client, and banking data are omitted;
- the reservation credit and remaining annual balance are understood;
- the 30-day price lock begins only after the stated activation conditions are met; and
- public submission alone creates no commercial obligation.
