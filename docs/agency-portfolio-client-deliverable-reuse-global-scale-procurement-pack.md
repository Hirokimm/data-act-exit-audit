## Permitted derived outputs

Subject to the accepted written scope, Reuse Global Scale may cover:

- client-specific written reports or memos;
- client-specific presentations;
- derived charts or tables; and
- internal advisory workpapers supporting a client deliverable.

The licensee remains responsible for its client relationships, professional judgments, representations, and final deliverables.

## Excluded uses

Reuse Global Scale does not permit:

- redistribution of the raw dataset;
- sublicensing;
- resale of dataset access;
- a white-label data portal or API;
- bulk public republication;
- use for more than 5,000 named end-client organizations without a separately accepted scope; or
- use outside the accepted written scope.

It does not add organizations to the dataset, custom research, implementation work, or a faster delivery cadence.
