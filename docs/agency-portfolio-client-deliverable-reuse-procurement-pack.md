# Agency Portfolio License Client-Deliverable Reuse Add-on — Procurement Pack

Use this page to prepare an internal approval, purchase order, or invoice request for annual rights to incorporate derived insights from an Agency Portfolio License into client-specific deliverables.

## Commercial summary

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| SKU | APL-CLIENT-REUSE-25 |
| Product | Client-Deliverable Reuse Add-on |
| Annual price | JPY 298,000 |
| Capacity | Up to 25 named end-client organizations during the annual term |
| Eligibility | Active Agency Portfolio License or base license procured in the same transaction |
| Delivery effect | Adds client-deliverable reuse rights to the existing licensed dataset and delivery cadence |
| Additional dataset production | None |
| Custom research | Not included |

Taxes, payment timing, effective date, renewal, cancellation, refund treatment, named-client scope, and accepted outputs follow the mutually accepted written terms.

## Permitted derived outputs

Subject to the accepted written scope, the add-on may cover:

- client-specific written reports or memos;
- client-specific presentations;
- derived charts or tables; and
- internal advisory workpapers supporting a client deliverable.

The licensee remains responsible for its client relationships, professional judgments, representations, and final deliverables.

## Excluded uses

The add-on does not permit:

- redistribution of the raw dataset;
- sublicensing;
- resale of dataset access;
- a white-label data portal or API;
- bulk public republication;
- use for more than 25 named end-client organizations without a separately accepted scope; or
- use outside the accepted written scope.

It does not add organizations to the dataset, custom research, or a faster delivery cadence.

## Internal approval checklist

- [ ] Base-license status is active or included in the same procurement.
- [ ] Expected annual end-client band is 1–5, 6–15, or 16–25 organizations.
- [ ] Intended deliverable types are identified.
- [ ] Requested start and end dates align with the base-license term.
- [ ] Raw-data redistribution, sublicensing, access resale, and white-label portal/API use are excluded.
- [ ] Procurement route is selected: invoice without PO, purchase order, or route pending.
- [ ] Billing, client names, and confidential details will be exchanged privately only.
- [ ] Final scope, tax, written terms, and payment requirements will be confirmed before activation.
