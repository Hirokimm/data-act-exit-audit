# Client-Deliverable Reuse Global — Reservation Tier Selection Guide

Use this public buyer guide to select the smallest reservation tier that covers your expected annual number of named end-client organizations.

This guide is for the 501–20,000 named-client range. It is not a quote, contract, invoice, reservation, price lock, license grant, or guarantee. Eligibility, scope, accepted outputs, written terms, tax, procurement route, and payment details must be confirmed privately.

The per-organization figures are illustrative arithmetic based on the annual price divided by the lower or upper end of each intended band. Lower-bound figures are rounded upward. Buyers must use their own forecast and approved assumptions.

## Selection rule

Choose the smallest tier whose maximum covers the number of distinct named end-client organizations you reasonably expect to serve during the annual term.

- 501–1,000: Global 1K
- 1,001–2,500: Global Plus 2.5K
- 2,501–5,000: Global Scale 5K
- 5,001–10,000: Global Network 10K
- 10,001–20,000: Global Network 20K
- More than 20,000: request separate written scope; do not assume a listed tier applies

Do not split one annual use case across multiple lower tiers to avoid the applicable named-client band. Named-client counting, affiliates, legal entities, duplicate organizations, term alignment, and permitted outputs must be confirmed in written terms.

## Common scope

Subject to the final annual order and accepted written terms, these tiers are intended for derived licensed-dataset insights in:

- client-specific written reports or memoranda;
- client-specific presentations;
- derived charts or tables; and
- internal advisory workpapers supporting client deliverables.

They do not permit raw dataset redistribution, sublicensing, resale of dataset access, a white-label data portal or API, bulk public republication, or use outside accepted written scope.
