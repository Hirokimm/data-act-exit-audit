# Agency Portfolio License Expansion Add-ons — Procurement Pack

Use this page to prepare an internal approval, purchase order, or invoice request for annual Agency Portfolio License expansion rights.

## Available annual add-ons

| SKU | Add-on | Annual price (JPY) | What changes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| APL-USER-10 | Additional 10-user pack | ¥49,800 | Adds up to 10 authorized users to an eligible base license |
| APL-ENTITY-1 | Additional legal-entity usage right | ¥99,800 | Adds one eligible legal entity to the authorized usage scope |
| APL-EXP-BUNDLE | Expansion bundle | ¥129,800 | Adds up to 10 authorized users and one eligible legal entity |

Prices are annual. Taxes, payment timing, effective date, renewal, cancellation, and refund treatment follow the mutually accepted written terms.

## Eligibility and scope

- Buyer must hold an active Agency Portfolio License or be procuring the base license in the same transaction.
- An add-on expands usage rights only. It does not add organizations, custom research, a new dataset, or a faster update cadence.
- The base-license term and add-on term must be aligned in the written order.
- Public information is sufficient for the initial order request. Do not submit billing details, credentials, personal data, employer-internal data, or confidential information through the public form.
- Submitting the form does not create a contract or charge. Eligibility, written terms, and invoice or purchase-order handling are confirmed privately.
