Privacy

This MVP audits publicly accessible SaaS documentation. It does not require account credentials and does not intentionally collect private customer documents or personal data.

The scanner stores only public-source URLs, short evidence snippets, fetch metadata and conservative classifications required to reproduce the report. Raw fetched page bodies are not persisted by the scanner.

No cookies, localStorage, advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking are used by this public MVP. Any future analytics or monetization instrumentation must preserve the same privacy constraints unless this notice is updated first.

Do not submit credentials, private documents or personal data through the public issue tracker.
