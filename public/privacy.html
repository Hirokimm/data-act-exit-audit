Privacy

This MVP audits publicly accessible SaaS documentation. It does not require account credentials and does not intentionally collect private customer documents or personal data.

The scanner stores only public-source URLs, short evidence snippets, fetch metadata and conservative classifications required to reproduce the report. Raw fetched page bodies are not persisted by the scanner.

For minimum product measurement, the public landing page sends only one of four fixed event names — page view, report open, contact open or paid-pilot interest — to the Factory's Vercel-hosted KPI endpoint. The application event payload contains no page URL or path, query string, audited SaaS URL, page content, email address, cookie value, localStorage value, advertising identifier or user-created identifier.

No cookies, localStorage or advertising identifiers are used by this public MVP for its application-level KPI events. The hosting/analytics providers may process ordinary network metadata under their own infrastructure policies.

Do not submit credentials, private documents or personal data through the public issue tracker.
