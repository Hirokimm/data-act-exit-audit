How to read results

PASS means direct positive public evidence was found. REVIEW means potentially relevant evidence needs interpretation. UNKNOWN means no qualifying evidence was collected and must never be treated as non-compliance.

Why EU Data Act appears here

The EU Data Act is one reason SaaS switching, data export, interfaces and exit charges matter to European buyers and providers. We use it as context for the evidence categories, but this service does not certify legal compliance and does not replace contractual or legal review.

Scope

The scanner uses HTTPS-only public seed pages, bounded responses, timeouts, allowed-domain checks and no authentication bypass. Raw page bodies are not persisted; only short evidence snippets and metadata are retained.
